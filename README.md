# 📈 Stock Market Analysis – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing **stock market performance, company behavior, and price movement patterns** using **Power BI**. The goal is to transform raw market data into **interactive dashboards** that help users understand market trends, company performance, and stock behavior through visual analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
- Analyze overall **stock market trends and stability**
- Evaluate **individual company performance**
- Visualize **price movements and market behavior** using candlestick analysis
- Enable **interactive, data-driven exploration** for better market insights

---

## 📊 Data Collection
- Collected stock market data from **public financial websites**
- Integrated **live stock data** for near real-time analysis
- Used multiple datasets covering market-level and company-level information

---

## 🧱 Data Modeling & Preparation
- Cleaned and transformed raw data using **Power Query**
- Created **multiple related tables** for structured analysis
- Built relationships between tables to support:
  - Time-series analysis
  - Company-wise comparison
  - Market-level aggregation

---

## 📊 Dashboards Developed

### 1️⃣ Stock Market Dashboard
**Purpose:**  
Analyze overall market behavior and trend movement.

**Key Features:**
- Market-level trend analysis
- Performance comparison across time
- Interactive slicers for filtering

**Business Value:**  
Helps users understand **market stability, movement, and direction**.

---

### 2️⃣ Company Dashboard
**Purpose:**  
Evaluate performance of individual companies.

**Key Features:**
- Company-wise price trends
- Performance comparison between companies
- Interactive visuals linked with market data

**Business Value:**  
Supports **company-level analysis and comparison** for decision-making.

---

### 3️⃣ Scanlight Stock Dashboard (Candlestick Analysis)
**Purpose:**  
Visualize detailed stock price behavior.

**Key Features:**
- Candlestick charts (Open, High, Low, Close)
- Volatility and price movement patterns
- Interactive filtering across time and companies

**Business Value:**  
Provides deeper insight into **stock behavior and market sentiment**.

---

## 🔄 Interactivity
- All dashboards are **fully interactive**
- Filters and slicers affect visuals across dashboards
- Enables drill-down and comparative analysis

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Power Query
- Financial Market Data (Web Sources & Live Data)

---

## ✅ Key Outcomes
- Built a **multi-dashboard Power BI solution**
- Integrated live and historical stock data
- Delivered interactive dashboards for **market, company, and price analysis**
- Enabled clear visualization of complex financial data
